Auto-orchestrator

When enabled, Harbor scans every project and auto-pulls ready Backlog tasks into Planning. Agents then self-drive to Review; Done stays your manual acceptance gate. Admission is resource-blind — workers reserve runtime themselves (see the resource broker below).

No resources are declared. Workers discover what exists at the test boundary (e.g. adb devices) and acquire_runtime atomically locks the first free identity, so two tasks never collide on the same emulator / app instance. Busy → the task parks (FIFO) and is woken when one frees. Independent of admission — applies to manually-driven tasks too.
